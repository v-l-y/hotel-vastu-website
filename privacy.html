The current website is static. The booking enquiry form does not transmit or store your information on a server yet. It only prepares the details in your browser and asks you to contact the hotel to confirm availability.

If you call the hotel or use an external maps service, your interaction is handled by the relevant telephone provider or external service under its own privacy terms.

If an online form endpoint, analytics platform, payment service or WhatsApp integration is added later, this page must be updated before that feature is published.
